Privacy policy

Controller

Ola Vibes S.L., CIF B25889304, Calle El Hierro 9, 38612 El Médano, Spain. Email: mail@foilbattery.com (see also the legal notice).

Visiting the site

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address (server logs). Fonts and map libraries are hosted on this site itself, no Google Fonts are loaded. Your browser stores your login session locally so you stay logged in.

Map and place search

Your browser loads the map tiles from OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom). The provider receives your IP address and the visible map area. When you search for a place or set the pin while creating a listing, your browser sends the search text or the coordinates to the Nominatim service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation. We only ask for your device location when you tap "My location". It is not stored.

Account

For an account we store your email address, a hashed password and the time at which you confirmed the notice when signing up. Accounts and listings are stored at Supabase (region: London, United Kingdom; the European Commission has an adequacy decision for the United Kingdom). Your browser keeps the login in local storage so you stay logged in. We do not set additional cookies for advertising or analytics. Supabase sends the emails for confirming your address and resetting your password on behalf of the site.

Your listing

When you create a listing we store: display name, place name, the pin (randomly moved by 200 to 600 m if you wish), the batteries with prices, the deposit and whether an ID is shown, notes, your contact details (WhatsApp number and/or email).

  • Name, place, pin, batteries, prices, deposit and notes are visible to all visitors once approved. For schools and rentals this also applies to website and opening hours.
  • WhatsApp number and email are visible to logged-in members only.
  • "ID is shown at handover" is only a note for borrowers. We do not collect, store or check IDs. Whoever sees an ID processes that data on their own responsibility.

The legal basis is pre-contractual measures or your consent (Art. 6(1)(b) and (a) GDPR). You can pause, change or delete your listing at any time. When you delete it, the listing data is removed.

Reviewing new lenders

We review new listings by hand before they are published. For this we require the link to the Instagram or Facebook profile from private lenders and the website from schools and rentals (a profile is optional). We optionally accept an invoice from the manufacturer or a dealer. We look at the public profile or website to see whether foil assist batteries exist. When you submit an application, the site notifies us by email and includes the details from your application.

  • The invoice is stored in a private storage that only you and we can see. We delete it as soon as we have decided on your application.
  • The profile link is stored only for the review and is never visible to other users. The website of a school or rental, however, is public. It is deleted when you delete your listing.
  • After our decision you receive an email with the result, with a reason if the application is declined.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and fake listings, and pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(f) and (b) GDPR).

Sending emails

We send this site's emails (sign-up confirmation, notifications about applications and decisions) through our email account at IONOS via SMTP. IONOS processes the recipient address and the content of the email. Emails you send us are forwarded to a Google Mail mailbox (Google Ireland Ltd.) for handling.

Contact between members

If you use the "Message on WhatsApp" or "Send an email" buttons, your device opens WhatsApp or your mail app. From there the privacy terms of that service apply. Price, handover and payment happen entirely outside this site.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to the email address above. If you want your account deleted completely, contact us there as well. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).
