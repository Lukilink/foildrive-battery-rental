6. Battery safety

Foil assist batteries are powerful lithium-ion batteries. Lenders and borrowers should, as a minimum:

  • inspect the battery together at handover and document its condition (photos are a good idea);
  • never use or charge a battery that is damaged, swollen, overheated or smells unusual;
  • charge only with a suitable charger, on a non-flammable surface and not unattended;
  • store and transport the battery as the manufacturer and local rules require, away from heat and direct sun.

Lithium-ion batteries of this size are generally not allowed on passenger aircraft. Never try to take a borrowed battery on a flight.
