    How it works

    Foil assist batteries are not allowed on planes. So travellers borrow one on site, from locals, schools and rentals at the spot. This site is only the map. Everything else you arrange directly.

    You are travelling

    1. Find your destination. Filter by the brand and battery model you need.
    2. Log in and get in touch. Logged-in members see the contact details. Write via WhatsApp or email.
    3. Agree price, handover and deposit. Payment and the deal happen entirely outside this site.

    You have batteries to lend

    1. Create an account. Free. Email and password are enough.
    2. Submit your listing. Choose whether you lend privately or as a school / rental. Enter your batteries, location, contact and deposit. Private lenders add their Instagram or Facebook profile, schools and rentals their website and opening hours.
    3. Wait for approval. We review every listing briefly. You get an email, then your pin appears. You can pause or delete it any time.

    Good to know

    Verified lenders
    Every lender is checked briefly before approval: profile link or website and optionally an invoice. Only then does the pin appear on the map.
    Deposit and handover
    Each lender states a deposit and whether they want to see an ID. Still agree on the pick-up place and return time beforehand and take photos of the condition at handover.
    Liability
    This site only connects contacts. Lending and liability are solely between lender and borrower. Check beforehand whether your insurance applies.
    Privacy
    The pin can be shown roughly only. Contact details are visible to logged-in members only, and proofs only to us, only for the review.
    Partner badge
    Partners support this community project by linking to foilbattery.com on their website or social media. As a thank you they get the blue Partner badge, a slightly larger pin and are listed first. Want to become a partner? Add a link and send it to mail@foilbattery.com.

    Questions

    Can I take my foil assist battery on a plane?

    Usually not. Foil assist batteries are lithium-ion packs far above what airlines allow (typically 100 Wh, or 160 Wh with airline approval). That is why riders borrow a battery at their destination instead.

    How does borrowing a battery work?

    Search your destination on the map and filter by brand and battery model. Log in to see the lender's contact details, then message them via WhatsApp or email. Price, deposit and handover are agreed directly between you.

    Does it cost anything to use the site?

    No. Searching, listing and contacting lenders is free. This site takes no fees and handles no payments.

    Which brands and batteries can I find?

    Foil Drive, Manta Foils, RIIFT, Duotone, Flow Customs, Flite, Waydoo and other foil assist and eFoil batteries. Lenders can also add models that are not in the list.

    How do I list my battery, school or rental?

    Create a free account, choose private or school / rental, and add your batteries, location and contact details. We review every listing by hand before it appears on the map.

    Who runs this site?

    An unofficial community project by foil assist riders, operated by Ola Vibes S.L. in El Médano, Tenerife. It is not affiliated with any manufacturer.
